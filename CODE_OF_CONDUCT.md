# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be patient, and remember that everyone here is learning, including the maintainers.
Criticise code, never people.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming, especially to people newer than you. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. "This will break if the list is empty" is
  helpful. "You clearly did not think about this" is not.
- Explain your reasoning when you leave a review comment, so the person learns something
  instead of just fixing one line.
- It is fine to disagree. Do it kindly, and be willing to change your mind too.
- Ask questions freely. Nobody will think less of you for not knowing something.

## What is not okay

- Insults, put-downs, or making fun of someone's questions or skill level
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Sexual comments or imagery
- Anything that would make someone feel unwelcome in a learning space

## About code review specifically

Reviews here can be pretty direct. Someone might tell you a pull request needs a lot of
rework, or that an approach will not work. That is normal and it is not a violation of this
document. The line is simple: comments about the *code* are fine, however blunt. Comments
about *you* are not.

## If something happens

Tell a maintainer, listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), or reach out to the ACM IIT
Mandi chapter directly. We will keep it private and handle it fairly. If the maintainer list
still says "add contact here", that means this has not been filled in yet, please ping the
chapter directly in the meantime.
