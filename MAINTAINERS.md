## How to reach us

Open an issue or a [Discussion](https://github.com/ACM-IIT-Mandi/Stack-Blueprint/discussions),
or tag a maintainer in a pull request. For anything sensitive, message the ACM IIT Mandi core
team directly, or use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security problems specifically.

## Becoming a maintainer

There's no application. If you've had a few pull requests merged and you've been helpful in
reviews, someone will ask you. If you think you've been missed, just say so, that's a
completely reasonable thing to bring up.

## Making decisions

Small stuff: one maintainer approves and merges.

Bigger stuff, like changing the standards or the folder layout: open an issue, give people a
few days to respond, then go ahead if nobody objects.

If we disagree and can't settle it, the maintainers talk it out and take a majority view.

## Handover

Everyone graduates eventually, so before you leave:

1. Find someone to take over what you look after, and update the table above.
2. Write a short note in an issue: what's done, what's half done, what you'd do next.
3. Add the new person as a collaborator on GitHub (see [REPO_GUIDE.md](REPO_GUIDE.md)).

Try to overlap by a few weeks rather than handing over on your last day. A project that goes
quiet for a summer is hard to restart.
